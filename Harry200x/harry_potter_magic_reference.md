# Harry Potter Magic Reference

A compact catalogue of the spells, potions and other branches of magic in the Wizarding World.
Source tags: **B** = the seven books, **F** = films, **FB** = Fantastic Beasts films, **P** = Pottermore / Wizarding World site, **G** = video games, Wonderbook: Book of Spells or trading cards. Unmarked entries are from the books.

Spell types: **Charm** adds or changes a property (levitate, lock, cheer). **Transfiguration** changes what a thing *is*. **Jinx** = minor irritant, **Hex** = moderate dark magic, **Curse** = the worst. **Counter-spell** undoes another spell. **Healing** repairs the body.

| **Anti-Cheating Spell** | Charm | Placed on exam quills so they cannot be used to cheat. |
| **Anti-Disapparition Jinx** | Jinx | Stops anyone in an area from Disapparating; Dumbledore trapped Death Eaters with it. |
| **Aparecium** (Revealing Charm) | Charm | Makes invisible ink or hidden writing appear. |
| **Appare Vestigium** (FB) | Charm | Reveals recent tracks and traces of magic as golden images. |
| **Arania Exumai** (F) | Charm | Blasts spiders away from the caster. |
| **Arresto Momentum** (F) | Charm | Slows or stops a falling object or person. |
| **Ascendio** (F) | Charm | Propels the caster upward, out of water or into the air. |
| **Avada Kedavra** (Killing Curse) | Unforgivable Curse | Jet of green light causing instant death. No counter-curse; Harry is the only known survivor. |
| **Avis** | Charm | Conjures a flock of small birds from the wand (Ollivander testing Krum's wand). |
| **Avifors** (G) | Transfiguration | Turns a small object or statue into a bird. |

### Spells known by effect only (no incantation given)
- **Bluebell flames**: Hermione's portable, waterproof blue fire that can be carried in a jar.
- **Cross-species Switches and Vanishing**: standard fifth-year Transfiguration.
- **Ear-Shrivelling Curse, Horn-Tongue Hex, Jelly-Fingers, Jelly-Legs Jinx, Knee-Reversal Hex, Pimple Jinx, Toenail-Growth Hex, Twitchy-Ears Hex**: schoolyard hexes that do exactly what the names say.
- **Hair-Thickening Charm**: Snape's sarcastic explanation for Alicia Spinnet's eyebrows.
- **Imperturbable Charm**: seals a door against sound, objects and Extendable Ears (Mrs Weasley on the kitchen door).
- **Hour-Reversal Charm**: the magic inside a Time-Turner; safe only for a few hours back.
- **Shield-type enchantments on Hogwarts**: Unplottable, Anti-Apparition, Muggles see ruins with a danger sign.
- **Thief's Downfall**: Gringotts waterfall that strips enchantments and disguises.
- **Transmogrifian Torture**: Lockhart's invented diagnosis for Mrs Norris.
- **Flight without a broom**: Voldemort and Snape are the only wizards shown to fly unsupported.
- **Non-verbal and wandless magic**: sixth-year skill (non-verbal) and rare adult skill (wandless); children, house-elves and goblins do wandless magic naturally.

## 3. Other branches and kinds of magic

### Taught disciplines
- **Charms**: adds, removes or changes properties without changing identity (Flitwick).
- **Transfiguration**: changes what something is. Branches: Transformation, Vanishment (Evanesco), Conjuration (making things from nothing), Untransfiguration, Human Transfiguration (McGonagall). Governed by strict laws; food cannot be conjured, only summoned or multiplied.
- **Defence Against the Dark Arts**: counter-curses, duelling, dark creatures, shield and Patronus work.
- **Potions**: brewing; a subtle art where precision and timing matter more than wand power.
- **Herbology**: care and use of magical plants (Mandrakes, Devil's Snare, Gillyweed, Venomous Tentacula, Bubotubers, Snargaluffs, Whomping Willow).
- **Care of Magical Creatures**: handling beasts from Flobberworms to Hippogriffs.
- **Astronomy**: star and planet charts; feeds into Divination and Potions timing.
- **History of Magic**, **Muggle Studies**: non-practical.
- **Divination**: predicting the future via tea leaves, crystal balls, palmistry, fire-omens, astrology, dream interpretation, cartomancy. Mostly imprecise; true Seers (Trelawney, Cassandra Trelawney) are rare and their prophecies are stored as glass orbs in the Hall of Prophecy.
- **Arithmancy**: predicting via numbers; Hermione's favourite.
- **Study of Ancient Runes**: translating runic script.
- **Alchemy**: transmutation of matter; Nicolas Flamel's Philosopher's Stone turns metal to gold and yields the Elixir of Life. Offered at N.E.W.T. level if demand exists.
- **Apparition**: instant teleportation. Licensed at 17; the "Three D's" (Destination, Determination, Deliberation); botched attempts cause Splinching (body parts left behind); Side-Along Apparition carries a passenger; blocked at Hogwarts, Azkaban and Gringotts; house-elves can Apparate anywhere.

### Mind and memory magic
- **Legilimency**: extracting thoughts, memories and feelings from another mind. Eye contact and a wand help; Voldemort, Snape and Dumbledore are masters.
- **Occlumency**: closing the mind against Legilimency and the Imperius Curse by emptying it of emotion. Snape taught Harry; Draco learnt from Bellatrix.
- **Memory modification**: the Memory Charm (Obliviate), False Memory Charms (Slughorn edited his own), the Ministry's Obliviators. Memories can be stored in vials and viewed in a **Pensieve**; tampered memories look thick and cloudy.
- **Confundus**: temporary confusion rather than lasting change.
- **Imperius**: absolute control; see Unforgivable Curses below.

### Transformation of the self
- **Animagus**: a wizard who learns to turn into one specific animal at will, keeping their mind. Must register with the Ministry (McGonagall cat, Sirius dog, James stag, Pettigrew rat, Rita Skeeter beetle; the last four unregistered). The process (P) involves holding a Mandrake leaf in the mouth for a month, a potion, and waiting for a thunderstorm. Markings on the body show on the animal.
- **Metamorphmagus**: born ability to change appearance at will (Tonks, Teddy Lupin). Cannot be learnt.
- **Werewolf**: a curse spread by bite; involuntary transformation at full moon and loss of mind, managed only by Wolfsbane Potion.
- **Maledictus** (FB): a blood curse carried through the female line that eventually turns the victim permanently into a beast (Nagini).
- **Obscurial / Obscurus** (FB): a child who suppresses their magic produces a destructive parasitic force; usually fatal before age ten (Credence Barebone the exception).
- **Polyjuice** and **Transfiguration** cover temporary changes; **Human Transfiguration** gone wrong sends you to St Mungo's.

### Inborn or rare gifts
- **Parseltongue**: speaking to and understanding snakes; hereditary in Slytherin's line; Harry had it through the Horcrux inside him.
- **Seer**: true prophecy; cannot be learnt.
- **Legilimency** and **Occlumency** can be learnt, but natural talent varies.
- **Veela charm**: part-Veela women (Fleur) entrance men without casting.
- **Squibs**: born to wizards but without magic (Filch, Arabella Figg), the mirror of **Muggle-borns**.
- **Love-based protection**: Lily's sacrifice placed a lingering protection in Harry's blood, which Voldemort's rebirth potion then copied into himself.

### The Dark Arts
- **Unforgivable Curses**: Avada Kedavra (death), Crucio (torture), Imperio (control). Using any on a human earns a life sentence in Azkaban; Barty Crouch Sr. briefly authorised Aurors to use them.
- **Horcrux**: an object hiding a fragment of the maker's soul, anchoring them to life. Requires committing murder and a spell "too horrible" to print. Voldemort made seven (diary, Gaunt ring, Slytherin's locket, Hufflepuff's cup, Ravenclaw's diadem, Nagini, and Harry by accident). Destroyed only by things beyond magical repair: Basilisk venom, Fiendfyre, the Sword of Gryffindor, the Killing Curse on a living Horcrux. A Horcrux can possess and drain anyone who confides in it.
- **Inferi**: corpses reanimated by a Dark wizard as mindless guards; cold and water-loving; repelled by fire and light.
- **Dark Mark**: Voldemort's skull-and-serpent sign, cast in the sky by Morsmordre and branded on his followers' forearms as a summoning link (a Protean Charm).
- **Taboo**: see spells; the trace on "Voldemort".
- **Fiendfyre**, **Sectumsempra**, **Confringo**, **Expulso**: offensive Dark spells listed above.
- **Cursed objects**: Marvolo's ring (would have killed Dumbledore), the opal necklace (Katie Bell), the Hand of Glory (light only for the holder), the Vanishing Cabinet, the Flagrante and Gemino-cursed treasure, Moody's eye, Borgin and Burkes stock. Curse-Breakers (Bill Weasley) disarm them for Gringotts.
- **Blood magic**: the Gringotts and Grimmauld Place blood wards, the blood pact between young Dumbledore and Grindelwald that stopped them fighting (FB), and the sacrificial protection on Privet Drive that lasted until Harry turned 17.
- **Dementor's Kiss**: not a spell but the soul-sucking act of a Dementor; leaves an empty living body. Dementors feed on happiness and are repelled only by the Patronus.

### Binding magic and contracts
- **Unbreakable Vow**: a sworn promise sealed by a Bonder's wand; breaking it means death (Snape's vow to Narcissa).
- **Magical contracts**: the Goblet of Fire's binding entry; the enchanted D.A. sign-up parchment (Marietta's SNEAK pimples).
- **Fidelius Charm**, **Secret-Keepers** and **Taboo**: see spells.
- **Life debt**: a wizard whose life is saved is magically bound in some way to the saviour (Pettigrew's hesitation, Snape's attitude to James).
- **Wand allegiance**: a wand "chooses" its owner and can switch loyalty when its master is defeated or disarmed. The Elder Wand follows this rule absolutely.

### Death, souls and the afterlife
- **Ghosts**: imprints of departed wizards who feared death and chose to stay; cannot touch or taste, can pass through objects; Muggles cannot become ghosts.
- **Portraits**: magical paintings holding an impression of the subject's personality and catchphrases; can move between their own frames.
- **The Veil** in the Department of Mysteries: an archway between life and death; those who hear whispers have seen death.
- **Resurrection Stone**: recalls shades of the dead, who are not fully alive and want to return.
- **Priori Incantatem**: when wands sharing a core (Harry's and Voldemort's Fawkes-feather wands) are forced to duel, one regurgitates its past spells as ghost echoes.
- **Thestrals**: visible only to those who have witnessed death.

### Wandlore
- A wand is a channel; the wand chooses the wizard. Woods (holly, yew, elder, vine, ash, hawthorn and others) and cores (phoenix feather, dragon heartstring, unicorn hair; rarely Veela hair or Thestral tail hair) shape its temperament.
- **Broken or borrowed wands** work poorly; **Ron's Spellotaped wand** backfired constantly.
- **The Trace**: Ministry charm that detects magic performed near anyone under 17.
- **Wand-making** and **wand lore** are Ollivander's and Gregorovitch's arts.

### Enchanted and magical objects (brief)
Marauder's Map (Homonculous Charm, insult-answering), Invisibility Cloak (Hallow version never fades), Mirror of Erised (shows deepest desire), Pensieve, Time-Turner, Sorting Hat (Legilimency-like reading), Goblet of Fire (impartial judge), Sneakoscope (whistles near untrustworthy people), Foe-Glass (shows approaching enemies), Secrecy Sensor and Probity Probe (detect concealment and dark objects), Deluminator (captures lights, later guides Ron home), Remembrall (red smoke when you forget something), Two-Way Mirror, Howler, Omnioculars, Vanishing Cabinets, Portkeys, Floo Network and Floo Powder, Knight Bus, Flying Ford Anglia, the Weasley family clock, Extendable Ears, Quick-Quotes Quill, Spell-Checking Quill, Self-Stirring Cauldron, Ever-Bashing Boomerangs, Fanged Frisbees, the Hogwarts moving staircases, the Room of Requirement (appears equipped for the asker's need), the Chamber of Secrets, the Deathly Hallows (Elder Wand, Resurrection Stone, Cloak).

### Creature-based magic
- **Phoenix**: tears heal any wound, carries immense weight, reborn from ashes, song gives courage.
- **Unicorn**: blood keeps you alive at the cost of a cursed half-life; hair for wands; horn and hair in potions.
- **Basilisk**: gaze kills; a reflected gaze Petrifies; venom destroys Horcruxes; fears the rooster's crow.
- **Mandrake**: cry fatal when mature; restores the Petrified.
- **Gillyweed**: grows gills and webbing for an hour.
- **Devil's Snare**: strangles; hates light and warmth.
- **House-elves**: powerful wandless magic (Apparition through Hogwarts wards) bound to a family until given clothes.
- **Goblins**: masters of metalwork; goblin-made silver absorbs only what strengthens it (the Sword of Gryffindor took in Basilisk venom).
- **Giants**: natural resistance to spells, like dragon hide.
- **Boggart**: shape-shifts into your worst fear.
- **Swooping Evil** (FB): venom, diluted, erases bad memories, used to Obliviate New York.
- **Qilin** (FB): sees into souls and bows to the pure of heart.

### Magical law and limits
- **International Statute of Wizarding Secrecy** (1692): magic hidden from Muggles.
- **Decree for the Reasonable Restriction of Underage Sorcery**: no magic outside school before 17, except in life-threatening situations.
- **Gamp's Law of Elemental Transfiguration**: five exceptions, chiefly that food cannot be created from nothing.
- **Love, death and the soul** cannot be undone: no spell truly resurrects the dead, no potion creates real love, and tampering with time is lethal.
