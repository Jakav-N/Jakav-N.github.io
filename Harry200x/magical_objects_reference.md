# Magical Objects and Other Magical Things in Canon

A working list for the story: what exists, what it does, where it comes from, and whether Harry would want it. Sources are the seven books unless marked (F film, FB Fantastic Beasts, P Pottermore/Wizarding World). "Want" is a rough rating of usefulness to a Harry who is short of input, output and hours and long on everything else.

---

## 1. Time

| Thing | What it does | Canon | Want |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Time-Turner** | Hourglass on a chain; each turn sends the wearer back one hour. Ministry controlled (Department of Mysteries), issued to Hermione in 1993 on McGonagall's vouching. Rules of use: never be seen by your past self, never change what you have already seen happen. The whole Ministry stock is destroyed in 1996. | PoA, OotP | Very high. The one object that buys real hours. Hard to get: he must learn they exist, then persuade the Ministry or Dumbledore. |
| **Hour-Reversal Charm** (P) | The enchantment inside a Time-Turner; unstable past about five hours. | P | Research target, not a purchase. |
| **Pensieve** | Stone basin; memories drawn out as silver thread can be re-entered and watched from inside, with perfect detail, including things the owner did not notice at the time. Dumbledore's is very old and Hogwarts property. Snape has one in his office. | GoF, OotP, HBP | High for others' memories (Lupin's, Dumbledore's, witnesses'). Less for his own: he already has them. |
| **Omnioculars** | Brass binoculars that replay, slow down and annotate what they have seen. Ten Galleons at the World Cup. | GoF | High as a prototype. A device that re-presents what it has recorded, at a chosen rate, is the ancestor of any reading device he builds. |
| **Deluminator** | Dumbledore's lighter-shaped device that captures and releases lights, and later guides Ron home by voice. One of a kind. | PS, DH | Curiosity. Not for sale. |

## 2. Reading, writing and record

| Thing | What it does | Canon | Want |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick-Quotes Quill** | Rita Skeeter's acid-green quill writes by itself what the speaker says, embellished. | GoF | A dictation quill exists; a plain one would write at speech pace, far below his need. Useful only for others. |
| **Self-inking, spell-checking, auto-answer quills** | Sold in Diagon Alley and Hogsmeade; auto-answer quills are banned in exams. Spell-checking quills wear off. | OotP, HBP | Low. Proof that quills take charms well. |
| **Anti-Cheating quills** | Used in O.W.L.s. | PS | Low. |
| **Riddle's diary** | Enchanted book that answers in writing, absorbs ink, shows memories. The enchantment on the book is a Horcrux, but the writing-back is ordinary dark enchanting of a text object. | CoS | Soul magic is a wall; the book-that-answers idea is not. |
| **Marauder's Map** | Shows the castle and every person in it by name, in real time. Homonculous Charm. Insults Snape. Confiscated by Filch in the 1970s; in his office until the twins take it in 1989 or so. | PoA | High, and it is sitting in Filch's filing cabinet while Harry lives at Hogwarts. Shows "Peter Pettigrew" when the rat is in the building. |
| **Moving photographs** | Developed in a special solution (Colin Creevey). Subjects move within the frame and can leave it; portraits speak, photographs do not. | CoS | Low. |
| **Enchanted portraits** | Hold an impression of the sitter, speak, move between their own frames anywhere. Headmasters' portraits advise. Sir Cadogan, the Fat Lady, Phineas Nigellus. | throughout | Medium. Portraits are a message network and a store of dead experts' knowledge, but their understanding is shallow. |
| **Howler** | Red envelope that screams its letter aloud and burns. | CoS | None. |
| **Hogwarts library catalogue** | Not described; Madam Pince's charms keep books in order and bite thieves. The Restricted Section is behind a grille, books scream if opened wrongly. | PS, HBP | He will want to know how Pince's ordering charms work. |
| **Books that act** | The Monster Book of Monsters (bites until stroked), the Invisible Book of Invisibility, books in the Restricted Section that scream. | PoA, PS | Shows that books carry charms well: a reading device could be a book-side charm rather than a glass. |

## 12. Laws and institutions that bear on objects

- **Misuse of Muggle Artefacts Office** (Arthur Weasley): enchanting Muggle-made things is illegal. His mail-order idea must sell them plain.
- **Statute of Secrecy**: nothing magical to Muggles.
- **Decree for the Reasonable Restriction of Underage Sorcery** and **the Trace**: not watching him until the letter.
- **Registry of Proscribed Charmable Objects**: carpets are on it.
- **Ministry patents**: Horton-Keitch Braking Charm is patented; so inventions can be owned.
- **Goblin property law**: goblin-made things return to the maker on the buyer's death, by their lights. Do not buy goblin silver expecting to keep it.
- **Wizengamot, Department of Magical Law Enforcement, Improper Use of Magic Office, Department of Mysteries, Department of International Magical Co-operation, Department for the Regulation and Control of Magical Creatures**: the whole state is a few hundred people in one building.

## 13. Things he cannot have and should know about

- **The Deathly Hallows**: Elder Wand (Dumbledore's), Resurrection Stone (in Marvolo's ring, a Horcrux, in the Gaunt shack), the Cloak (his).
- **The Philosopher's Stone**: Flamel's; comes to Hogwarts in 1991 unless events change.
- **Horcruxes**: diary (Malfoy Manor), ring (Gaunt shack), locket (Grimmauld Place, with Kreacher), cup (Lestrange vault), diadem (Room of Requirement), Nagini (later), himself. Soul magic is a wall in this story; these take a very long time.
- **The prophecy** about him, in the Hall of Prophecy and in Dumbledore's and Snape's heads.
