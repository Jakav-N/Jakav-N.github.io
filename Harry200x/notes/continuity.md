# Continuity ledger

Update after every chapter. Facts fixed in prose are binding on later chapters.

## Dates
- Harry James Potter born 31 July 1980. Dudley Dursley born 23 June 1980.
- Scar night: 31 October 1981. Harry left on the step at 4 Privet Drive the next night; Petunia finds him the following morning.

## People
- Vernon Dursley: director at Grunnings (drills). Petunia Dursley. Dudley. Marge Dursley visits; bulldogs.
- Arabella Figg: Squib; Wisteria Walk, two streets from Privet Drive; cats Mr Tibbles (big, flat-faced, yellow eyes; stares), Snowy, Mr Paws (not well), Tufty (shy, under the sideboard); house smells of cabbage and cats, gas fire, photographs of cats on every surface including the television; string bag; ordinary voice dry and a little cracked. Reports to Dumbledore. Dropped the baby-talk voice after Harry spoke to her and never used it again.
- Yvonne: Petunia's friend, minded Dudley the night of the Grunnings dinner (Dec 1982).
- Albus Dumbledore: great ally from chapter 4. Condition: at least three weeks a year at Privet Drive.
- Minerva McGonagall: Harry saw her turn into a cat on the night he arrived (every stage visible to him).
- Hagrid: carried Harry from Godric's Hollow; wept.
- Sirius Black: held Harry at Godric's Hollow; Harry keeps his face exactly. Not yet named to Harry.
- Remus Lupin: enters chapter 6.

## The house
- 4 Privet Drive: front room with television, kitchen, hall with cupboard under the stairs, four bedrooms (Vernon and Petunia; Dudley; Dudley's second room full of junk where Harry's cot is from Nov 1981; guest room for Marge).
- Harry: cot in the junk room by night, playpen in the front room by day (Nov 1981 onward). Moves to the cupboard under the stairs in autumn 1982 (chapter 2).
- Books in the house (to be fixed as they appear): Ladybird Key Words readers bought for Dudley; The Very Hungry Caterpillar; Collins dictionary; a world atlas; Children's Britannica (bought Jan 1983); Petunia's cookery books; Vernon's Reader's Digest condensed novels; the Highway Code; Yellow Pages; Argos catalogue; Radio Times; the Daily Mail daily.
- Television: ordinary set until May 1982; then a Teletext set bought by Vernon "for the cricket and the share prices". Old set stands in the junk room, flex wound, facing the wall. Petunia leaves subtitles on most evenings out of habit.
- Junk room boxes labelled Xmas Decs, Dudley Baby Clothes, Marge's Things Do Not Throw. Jar of buttons and beads (now cracked, hidden). Ladybird books and a torn old catalogue in the box by the window.
- Cupboard: width of the mattress, stairs for ceiling, a spider in the high corner. Bolt outside.
- Petunia's bureau drawer in the front room: Dumbledore's 1971 reply to her, and his 1981 letter (chapter 3).

## Harry's capabilities by date
- Nov 1981: five words (Mummy, Daddy, cat, no, up). Can hold a spoken word whole and compare it. Sees scan lines, falling dust, the edge of moving light.
- Feb 1982: understands dozens of words from television and Petunia; matches some printed words from the Ladybird books to their sounds. First magic at Privet Drive: the bottle comes to him (pictured, not merely wanted).
- Apr 1982: first deliberate words, "Please" and "Water, please", in the high chair. Speaks only when it buys something. Dudley's first word "won't" a fortnight later.
- Jun–Jul 1982: reading cracked from Ceefax subtitles (page found by Petunia in June). Reads the Daily Mail, cereal box, Radio Times, the Argos-type catalogue (numbers and sums from prices). Can climb out of the cot. Read all twelve Ladybird books during Dudley's party.
- Autumn 1982: wandless, at night, pictured not wanted: lifts socks, beads, pages; objects move at a slow walking pace only; a jar he did not picture landing cracked (hidden under curtains); a steady light the size of an egg over his palm that lasts while he holds the picture. Books too heavy to lift; bigger things do nothing. He does not know why.
- Oct 1982: accidental magic in sleep after dreaming of the green light: boxes stacked against the junk-room door, bulb shattered. Moved to the cupboard under the stairs that evening (cot mattress on the floor, bolt fitted on the outside by Vernon). Can slide the bolt from inside by picturing it; has not yet gone out at night.
- Dec 1982: first evening at Mrs Figg's. Said "Harry Potter" aloud for the first time (Potter learned from Vernon's "That Potter was the same, the one time I met him"). Read to her from a cat book (Russian Blue). Does not know his own age or birthday. Overheard, through the kitchen wall: "He said to keep an eye on him. He never said anything about this. Dumbledore'll want to hear, Mr Tibbles. I shall have to write." Holds "Dumbledore" as a word, not yet connected to "that man".

## What the Dursleys know and have said
- Petunia read Dumbledore's letter on the step. She tells Marge (by telephone) that Harry's parents died in a car crash.
- After the bottle: Vernon, "one of them", "I won't have it in my house", "the first sign of any more of it and he goes in the cupboard"; Petunia, "that man said we had to keep him", "you read it".
- Petunia told Marge by telephone: "Car crash." Harry heard it; he has not yet any reason to doubt it.
- Vernon met James once ("That Potter was the same").
- Petunia told Figg "He's very quiet." Figg told Petunia she'd have him any time.

## Rules established on the page
- The world did not slow down; he sped up, inside only; his arm moves at the world's pace.
- Magic responds to a complete picture of the end state, not to wanting.
