# Master arc (coarse; the ten-chapter outlines are authoritative where they differ)

## Arithmetic that drives everything
- 200 subjective days per real day. About 200 subjective years per real year. By age three, roughly 250 subjective years lived; by the Hogwarts letter, roughly 2,000.
- Thinking is effectively instant. The limits are: eyes (saccades; reads at a fast human pace until he builds a device), hands and voice (child's body, normal speed), sleep, and access to books, a wand, ingredients and people.
- So the plot is a sequence of input bottlenecks and how he breaks each one: speech → reading → the house's books → Figg's house and the public library → Dumbledore's books → a wand → Hogwarts library → faster reading device → people and researchers → money and institutions.

## Real-time spine
- 1981–83 (ch 1–3): infant and toddler at Privet Drive. Language, reading, hidden small magic, the letters in Petunia's drawer, the decision that what matters to him is that things go well for people. Figg.
- 1983–85 (ch 4–10): Dumbledore, wand, books, Lupin as tutor, McGonagall, first summer at Hogwarts, output method (transfiguring written pages), the Prophet learns he exists, first letters, Neville. Lives at Figg's; three weeks a year at Privet Drive.
- 1985–88 (ch 11–20): Hogwarts library in earnest; reading device built with Flitwick from Omniocular-type charms; Snape and potions method; Wolfsbane programme; Figg's mail-order business as his first commercial front; Weasleys, Lovegoods (Pandora's spell experiments), Augusta and Neville; Parseltongue discovered and filed as a dead end (soul and mind magic are hard); Marauder's Map recovered from Filch's office; Scabbers identified as Pettigrew once Percy brings him to Hogwarts in 1987; Sirius freed 1988. First book published. Fred and George as collaborators on spell combination. First successful bound spell (small).
- 1988–91 (ch 21–35): a research house employing muggle-born graduates and werewolves; standardised potions; warding products; a methodology for safe spell creation (which saves Pandora Lovegood in 1990); the newspaper; muggle investments through a solicitor compounding modestly; Ministry attention; Fudge; Hermione's letter with corrections; Draco and Lucius handled politely; Quirrell's trip to Albania; the letter and the Trace.
- 1991–95 (ch 36–100): Hogwarts years one to three or four. Independent study and teaching role. Quirrell detected and contained; Voldemort's wraith stays a background threat. Chamber located in year one via Parseltongue and Myrtle; the basilisk negotiated with rather than killed; its venom gives Dumbledore his first Horcrux tool when the diary surfaces in 1992, which starts a hunt that stays slow. Azkaban reform and the destroying Patronus as the late climax. Ministry, press, employment, healing and education transformed by chapter 100. Voldemort still out there.

## Named threads to carry
- The locket stays with Kreacher and out of sight until much later. Soul magic is a wall; Harry hits it and files it.
- Dementor destruction only once his character has set, and in service of ending Azkaban as it is.
- He never lies. He does not reveal the number 200 to the public; those who know by 1991: Dumbledore, Figg, Lupin, McGonagall, Sirius, Flitwick, Pomfrey, Snape (deduced), Augusta (deduced).
- The Dursleys are treated decently and improved slightly by his presence; three weeks every summer.
- Names for inventions, companies, the newspaper and so on are not to be coined without asking Jacob first.
