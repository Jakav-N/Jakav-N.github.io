A Harry Potter fan novel

Harry Potter
and the Long Second

in one hundred chapters
On the night of the first of November, 1981, Albus Dumbledore left a sleeping child on a doorstep in Surrey, confident that he would grow up away from all that, quite ordinary, until the time came.

The child's mind ran two hundred times faster than the world.
By morning, he had been on that doorstep for two months.
