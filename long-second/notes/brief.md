# The brief — what the reader said, in order (verbatim where quoted)

## The opening message
1. Harry Potter
2. His nervous system is 200x speed.
3. The only unrealistic thing is Harry's speed
4. Because of his speed, he'll be doing stuff. He'll kinda become an EA, roughly
5. He's really smart, and in general doesn't make dumb decisions.
6. Realistically, he's gonna be inventing stuff because he's so fast. Magitech?
   Destroying dementors? Many more things are possible, more crazy things.
7. He ain't gonna waste time.
"Ask me questions about this Harry, until you can start the 100 chapter story."

## Answers to the questions (round 1)
- What "200x nervous system" means: **Mind only (200x thinking).**
- When it begins: **From birth / the scar night.**
- Tone/genre: **Rationalist (HPMOR-adjacent).**
- Secrecy and the big players: **"People are going to notice very very quickly
  that he's a genius, but not necessarily how it works."**

## Round 2
