# The Long Second — Magic System Bible

> **SPOILER WARNING.** Author's working notes.

## The core model

Magic is an ambient field that couples to **structured conscious intention** in
people carrying the inherited receptor trait (wizards). Harry will privately call
it *the field* (early childhood name: *the Answering*, because it answered need).

Every magical effect requires three components:

1. **Form** — a precise mental structure specifying the effect: what, where, how
   much, under which conditions it ends. Forms are the "source code" of magic.
2. **Channel** — coupling efficiency between mind and field. Wands are tuned
   channels (quality of match matters — Ollivander's trade is impedance-matching).
   Wandless magic is possible but couples weakly: order-of-magnitude losses.
3. **Impetus** — drive: emotional/volitional energy that powers the coupling.
   Physiological; it tires like a muscle. Children's "accidental magic" is huge
   impetus with *no deliberate form* — the field improvises off raw desire, hence
   the wild, need-shaped results (hair regrows, glass vanishes, child lands softly).

## Spells

A **spell** is a *standardized form* — engineered by some past genius, polished by
generations of use — keyed to an incantation + wand-movement that reliably evoke
the form in the caster's *subconscious*. Wizards don't hold the form consciously;
they couldn't. The incantation/gesture is a mnemonic trigger; training a spell is
training the association until the subconscious renders the form on cue.

Consequences (all consistent with canon observations):
- **Silent casting** works for advanced wizards: the association is strong enough
  to fire without the mnemonic. Proof that words aren't magic — *forms* are.
- **Wandless magic** is rare and weak: channel loss, not impossibility.
- Mispronunciation fails because the *association* misfires, not because the field
  speaks Latin.
- Spell creation is rare and dangerous: designing a form blind, at 1× speed, by
  trial and error, with your subconscious as the only compiler.
- "Power" differences between wizards = mostly impetus capacity + channel quality +
  cleanliness of rendered forms (talent = subconscious renders cleanly).

## Harry's edge (follows strictly from the premise)

Casting happens across ~a quarter-second of real time. For Harry that is ~50
subjective seconds. **He is the first wizard in history who can watch his own mind
cast in slow motion** — observe the form his subconscious renders, debug it,
eventually *construct forms consciously* with enormous precision and hold them
stable through the instant of release.

- Pre-wand: weak (no channel) but unprecedentedly *controlled* wandless effects.
  Ceiling in Act I: warmth, a latch nudged, a page turned, pain dulled. Nothing
  flashy. Reproducibility is the miracle, not magnitude.
- With a wand: precision nobody has seen (a feather that *holds station*), then
  **decompilation** — casting a standard spell slowly, reading the rendered form,
  transcribing it into his **form-notation** (a private symbolic language; taught
  to the Workshop later as "mnemonic diagrams" without revealing how he derived it).
- Then composition: parameterizing spells, splicing forms, eventually original
  engineering (Acts II–V).

## Limits (keep tension honest)

- Impetus is physiological: he tires, he can be exhausted; stamina trains slowly.
- Channel quality is partly innate; his is good, not divine.
- Rituals, runes, wards, potions = forms **encoded in matter/arrangement/time**
  rather than held in a mind — whole other engineering disciplines he must learn
  the slow way (this is where magitech comes from: encoded forms are
  *manufacturable*).
- The field has deep structure nobody understands (why conservation is violated
  only locally, what the exchange currency is) — open physics, source of late-game
  research plots. He does not solve the field; he engineers within it.
- 200× does not grant knowledge, only time. He can be wrong, surprised, outflanked
  by information asymmetry — just never out-thought in real time.

## Specific phenomena

- **The warming charm on the doorstep blanket** (ch. 1): Dumbledore's charm — a
  warmth that *answers need* (it modulates with the baby's temperature). Harry's
  first datum, pre-verbal, remembered as the Answering.
- **The Trace / underage detection**: area-and-signature based, not attributed —
  detects *rendered forms above a magnitude threshold* near a registered minor;
  ambient accidental magic in muggle homes is expected noise, auto-filed. Harry
  maps the threshold experimentally (ch. 13) and works under it. At Hogwarts the
  castle's ambient magic blankets everything (canon-consistent).
- **Perception filters** (Leaky Cauldron, Platform 9¾): standing encoded forms
  that nudge attention away. At 200×, Harry can catch his own attention *being
  moved* — he can't fully resist early (the push is upstream of consciousness),
  but he can detect and map the edges. Insight: the filters target the *process*
  of noticing, and his process has 200 gears where theirs expects one.
- **Legilimency/the Hat**: mind-to-mind interfaces run at the *mind's* clock — the
  Sorting Hat is the first entity that can keep up with him. Legilimency against
  Harry is like dipping a cup into a firehose: Snape/Dumbledore would get
  *something*, but he can flood, misdirect, and rotate decoy surface-thought
  faster than any legilimens can parse. (Occlumency for him = trivial-but-
  trainable; never total immunity, to keep stakes.)
- **Dementors**: stable, self-sustaining parasitic form-structures in the field —
  "spells that learned to eat." They feed on impetus (which is why victims feel
  drained and joyless). The **Patronus** is a blocking form (a decoy impetus-source
  of inedible shape). The **dissolving form** (Act III) breaks their
  self-maintenance recursion: derived from months of close observation. Unmaking
  one is not violence, it is *debugging* — which is why it can be done without an
  army.
- **Horcruxes**: consciousness has a field-side component (the *pattern*). A
  horcrux anchors a copy of a pattern to an object by a murder-powered encoding
  ritual. Patterns are detectable (to instruments, and — faintly — to Harry's
  slow-motion perception: the "doubled" feel of Quirrell, the diary's ping, and,
  eventually acknowledged, the cold grain in his own scar he walled off as a
  child).
- **Prophecy**: leave mechanics mysterious; the field has temporal structure
  nobody understands. Harry treats prophecy as data with unknown generator —
  respected, not obeyed.

## Naming/terminology discipline

- Harry's private terms: *the field*, *forms*, *channel*, *impetus*,
  *the Answering* (childhood), *form-notation*, *decompile* (he coins it after
  learning of compilers; pre-1991 he says *un-knotting*).
- Wizarding terms stay canonical in dialogue. The Workshop later blends both.
- Never let a wizard character use Harry's terms before being taught them.
